About us

Hurukuro Communications is a brand, experience, and growth consultancy.

Established in 2008, Hurukuro Communications is a through-the-line Marketing and Advertising consulting company. We focus on building brands and adding value to businesses using direct disciplines with executions across relevant media platforms to meet objectives.

These discussions and our story telling craft is based on data driven work to ensure that brands gain the necessary awareness and engagement to meet the desired business objectives. We aim to bring ideas to life and provide thoughtful sound solutions to business challenges.

Our personalised service, attention to detail and research-based approach allows us to achieve the best possible outcome for your brand while giving you the confidence that every solution is purposeful, relevant and aligned with your business objectives.
